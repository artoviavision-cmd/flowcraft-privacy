# FlowCraft Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 16, 2026

FlowCraft is a productivity Chrome extension that helps users organize prompts, manage queues, track progress, and save outputs within their own Google Flow workflow. This Privacy Policy explains what information FlowCraft processes, why it is used, and how it is protected.

## Information We Collect and Process

FlowCraft may process the following information to provide its core functionality:

- Google account sign-in information, such as your email address, when you choose to sign in.
- Authentication session tokens required to keep you signed in.
- Account usage information, such as your plan, daily usage count, remaining quota, subscription status, subscription ID, and current subscription period end date.
- Prompt queue, generation settings, workflow progress, gallery state, and language preference stored locally in your browser.
- Website content from Google Flow pages, such as prompts, generated media references, media URLs, project context, and generation status, only when needed to run the automation you start.
- Feedback information you submit, including your message, optional contact email, app version, account plan, recent error records, and technical context needed to investigate issues.

## Payment and Subscription Processing

Payments are intended to be processed by our payment provider. FlowCraft does not collect, store, or process credit card numbers or full payment card details.

FlowCraft stores limited subscription metadata in Supabase, such as plan, subscription status, subscription ID, and current period end date. This is used only to determine account access, quota, and subscription features inside the extension.

## How We Use Information

We use information only to:

- Authenticate users and manage account access.
- Track quota and subscription status.
- Submit and manage Google Flow image and video generation workflows.
- Collect generated media and support downloads requested by the user.
- Save local workflow settings and queue state.
- Receive and respond to feedback, bug reports, and support requests.
- Improve reliability and diagnose technical issues.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell user data.

We do not transfer user data to third parties for advertising, credit eligibility, or unrelated purposes.

FlowCraft communicates only with services required for its single purpose:

- Google Flow and Google APIs for generation workflows and media processing.
- Supabase for authentication, quota, subscription metadata, and feedback.
- Our payment provider for payment and subscription checkout.
- The configured upscaler endpoint for optional media upscaling.

## Local Storage

FlowCraft stores some information locally in Chrome storage and IndexedDB, including prompt queues, settings, gallery state, language preference, authentication session data, and cached account usage information. This helps the extension continue workflows reliably.

## Remote Code

FlowCraft does not execute JavaScript or WebAssembly from remote servers. All extension code is packaged with the extension. Network requests are used only for authentication, quota, feedback, generation, and media processing APIs.

## Data Retention

Local data remains in the browser until the user clears it, signs out, removes the extension, or clears browser storage.

Account, quota, subscription metadata, and feedback reports may be stored in Supabase as long as needed to provide the service, support users, maintain security, and comply with legal or operational requirements.

## User Control

Users can:

- Sign out from the extension to clear the local authentication session.
- Clear prompt queues, logs, and local workflow data inside the extension where available.
- Remove the extension from Chrome to delete extension-local data.
- Contact us to request deletion of account-related records or feedback submissions.

## Children's Privacy

FlowCraft is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated policy will be posted at the public privacy policy URL used in the Chrome Web Store listing.
