Refund Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

Short version: We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all new subscriptions, no questions asked.

1. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you are not satisfied with FlowCraft for any reason, you may request a full refund within 14 days of your initial purchase. This applies to monthly, yearly, and lifetime plans.

2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact us at support@flowcraft.support with your order details. Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days, depending on your payment method and bank.

3. Subscription Renewals

If you do not cancel your subscription before the renewal date, you will be charged for the next billing period. Refunds are not provided for renewal charges after the renewal date has passed, unless exceptional circumstances apply.

4. Cancellations

  • You may cancel your subscription at any time via the FlowCraft extension ("Manage Subscription" button) or directly through Paddle's customer portal.
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. You retain access to premium features until then.
  • Lifetime subscriptions cannot be cancelled as they are non-recurring one-time purchases.

5. Exceptions

We reserve the right to deny refunds in cases of abuse, such as repeated purchase-and-refund cycles, or violations of our Terms of Service.

6. Paddle as Payment Processor

Payments are processed by Paddle, who acts as the Merchant of Record. For billing disputes, you may also contact Paddle directly at paddle.com/help.

7. Contact

For refund requests or billing questions: support@flowcraft.support
