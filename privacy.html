Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

FlowCraft ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the FlowCraft Chrome extension and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide our services:

  • Account information: Email address and Google account ID, collected when you sign in with Google.
  • Usage data: Number of generations used per day, subscription status, and billing interval.
  • Payment information: Payments are processed by Paddle. We do not store credit card or bank account details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and manage your subscription.
  • To enforce daily generation limits for free tier users.
  • To process payments and manage billing through Paddle.
  • To provide customer support and respond to inquiries.

3. Data Storage

Account and usage data is stored securely on Supabase infrastructure (EU region). We retain your data as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google OAuth: Used for sign-in. We receive your email address and unique Google ID only.
  • Paddle: Our payment processor. Paddle's privacy policy applies to payment data. See paddle.com/legal/privacy.
  • Google Labs Flow: The extension interacts with Google Labs Flow using the user's own authenticated session. We do not store or transmit your generated content.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

The extension stores authentication tokens and preferences in Chrome's local storage (on your device only). We do not use tracking cookies.

6. Children's Privacy

FlowCraft is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of FlowCraft after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions: support@flowcraft.support
